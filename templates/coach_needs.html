what i actually need from a coach

The earnest version.

Not sure exactly what kind of coaching style works for me yet — hoping you can help me figure it out.

  • ⚖️
    Calibrated toughness
    Sometimes I'm too hard on myself in an annoying, counterproductive way. I need someone who knows when to push and when to say "you're fine, chill." Finding that balance is big.
  • 🧠
    Do I really need rest, or am I just a baby?
    I genuinely cannot always tell the difference. Help me understand when to go hard and when to actually rest — and help me not feel guilty about the latter.
  • 🗺️
    Mentorship & guidance
    Probably the biggest thing. I'm not just looking for workouts — I want to actually understand what I'm doing and why. I love to learn.
  • 🧩
    Help fitting it into real life
    I have a job, friends, family, a dog, and a personality. I need training that fits into a life I actually want to be living — not training that replaces it. Live, laugh, love, but in a swim bike run kind of way.
  • 🔄
    Finding balance (as best as possible)
    I know perfect balance is a myth. I just want to get closer to it. Whatever that looks like for this season of life.
